Products Built on Raiku

Raiku is not just a protocol upgrade but it enables a new class of products built around guaranteed execution and schedulable blockspace.

Bandwidth Markets

Bandwidth markets allow applications to purchase guaranteed execution capacity over time, rather than competing transaction-by-transaction.

This gives developers predictable performance, stable costs, and reliable user experience even during peak demand.

Why it matters: Apps can scale without breaking during congestion.

Global Accounts (Product View)

Global accounts act as shared coordination points that allow Raiku to enforce execution ordering and prevent conflicts across transactions.

As a product, global accounts enable complex workflows that require deterministic ordering and coordination.

Without Global Accounts

  • • Conflicting execution
  • • Unreliable ordering
  • • Complex retry logic

With Global Accounts

  • • Deterministic ordering
  • • Conflict prevention
  • • Reliable workflows

RFQ & Institutional Execution

Institutional workflows require exact execution timing, predictable settlement, and zero tolerance for retries.

Raiku enables on-chain RFQ (Request for Quote) execution by allowing transactions to be scheduled ahead of time with guaranteed inclusion.

Result: Institutions can deploy capital on-chain with confidence.

Execution Guarantees as a Product

Traditionally, blockchains sell access to blockspace. Raiku sells execution guarantees.

This shifts execution from a best-effort service into a first-class, market-priced product.

Why These Products Matter Together

Bandwidth markets, global accounts, and RFQ execution form a complete execution stack built on deterministic guarantees.

Together, they transform Solana from fast infrastructure into reliable infrastructure.
